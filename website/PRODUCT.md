# Aurora website

This public marketing site explains Aurora, a native iPhone/iPad assistant for
recording sleep and caffeine and reviewing estimates and reaction-test results.
The main action is to explore the synthetic preview; the app is not yet released.
Preserve the repository's product and privacy facts, planned price and release
status, explicit calendar approval, optional Health choices, and no-record rules.

The website belongs to Nathaniel Mapaye's visual family: nmapaye.com is the
primary reference. Its black, white, yellow editorial identity is website-only.
The native app and unchanged Aurora wave retain their own palette and behavior.
There are no app screenshots in this website source; the hero is explicitly
labeled a synthetic illustration. Do not present it as a device capture.
