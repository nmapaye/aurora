Aurora Support

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Getting Help

For non-sensitive bug reports, use the Aurora GitHub project. Do not post Health records, conversation content, calendar details, or credentials publicly.

Email: nmapaye@outlook.com for private support questions and beta feedback.

GitHub: github.com/nmapaye/aurora/issues

TestFlight Beta

To ask for an invite, email nmapaye@outlook.com with your device (iPhone, iPad or both) and iOS or iPadOS version.

Privacy: your email address is used only to send you a TestFlight invite and beta updates. It isn't sold, and it's shared only with Apple to send the invite. You can ask to be removed at any time.

Before Reporting

  • Include the Aurora version, iOS version, and device model.
  • Note whether Health access was granted, denied, or unavailable.
  • Do not include private Health data in public issue reports.

Known Limits

Aurora v0.1.0 is being prepared for iPhone and iPad. Cloud sync and Apple Watch support are not included. Optional suggestions refresh when the app is active and during background opportunities allowed by iOS; continuous background reasoning is not provided.
