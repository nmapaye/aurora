Aurora Support

Last updated: July 9, 2026

Getting Help

For non-sensitive bug reports, use the Aurora GitHub project. Do not post Health records, conversation content, calendar details, or credentials publicly. A private release-support contact must be confirmed before App Store submission.

GitHub: github.com/nmapaye/aurora/issues

Before Reporting

  • Include the Aurora version, iOS version, and device model.
  • Note whether Health access was granted, denied, or unavailable.
  • Do not include private Health data in public issue reports.

Known Limits

Aurora v0.1.1 is being prepared for iPhone and iPad. Cloud sync and Apple Watch support are not included. Optional suggestions refresh when the app is active and during background opportunities allowed by iOS; continuous background reasoning is not provided.
