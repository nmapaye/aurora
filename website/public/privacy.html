Privacy Policy

Aurora Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 9, 2026

Aurora is an iOS caffeine, sleep, and alertness app. It is designed to work without a cloud account. App data is stored on your device unless you explicitly share or export it.

Data Aurora Stores

  • Caffeine entries that you log manually.
  • Sleep sessions imported from Apple Health or entered through demo/sample data.
  • Vigilance reaction-test results.
  • Local preferences such as caffeine half-life, sleep target, appearance, and notification settings.

Apple Health

Aurora requests read-only access to recent sleep data from Apple Health. Health access is optional. Aurora does not write data to Apple Health and does not use Health data for advertising, marketing, or resale.

Storage and Sharing

Aurora stores app data locally on the device. Summary and CSV export features use the iOS share sheet and require explicit user action. The app does not create a cloud account or upload your logs to Aurora servers.

Deleting Data

You can remove demo/sample data in the app. You can also delete Aurora from your device to remove locally stored app data managed by iOS.

Medical Disclaimer

Aurora is informational only. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or condition.

Support

For privacy or support questions, use the support page or GitHub project linked from this site.
