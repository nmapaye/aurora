Privacy Policy

Aurora Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Aurora is a native iPhone and iPad assistant for energy and daily rhythm. It records caffeine and sleep, presents estimates and reaction-test results, and optionally reviews selected calendar commitments. Aurora has no cloud AI service or required Aurora account.

Data Aurora Stores

  • Caffeine entries that you log manually, and caffeine imported read-only from Apple Health if you turn that on.
  • Sleep sessions imported read-only from Apple Health or entered manually, plus explicitly labeled sample data.
  • Apple Health sync bookkeeping: read positions, the identifiers of caffeine samples Aurora added, and queued or failed Health changes.
  • Vigilance reaction-test results.
  • Local preferences such as caffeine half-life, sleep target, appearance, and notification settings.
  • Local conversation messages, dated response snapshots, and suggestion delivery/dismissal state.
  • Selected calendar identifiers and mirror preferences. Google OAuth tokens are stored in device-only Keychain storage.

Apple Health

Apple Health is optional and has three separate choices, each off until you turn it on: reading Sleep Analysis, reading dietary caffeine, and adding caffeine you save in Aurora to Apple Health. Records Aurora reads stay read-only in Aurora. When adding caffeine is on, Aurora saves each entry on your device first, then adds it to Apple Health, and updates or removes only samples Aurora itself wrote; it never changes data from other apps or devices. Turning it off stops further changes. A separate, optional "Update in the background" choice lets Apple Health wake Aurora when sleep or caffeine it reads changes; Apple Health decides when, and nothing leaves the device. Erasing Aurora's local data does not delete the samples Aurora already added to Apple Health; you can delete them in the Health app. Aurora does not use Health data for advertising, marketing, or resale.

Local Intelligence and Dictation

When available, Apple’s on-device Foundation Models classify requests into supported tasks. Deterministic app code supplies record values and calculations. Native quick actions remain available without a model or network connection. Optional dictation requires on-device speech recognition; Aurora does not upload audio to a cloud speech service. No analytics SDK or tracking is included.

Calendars

Calendar connections are optional. Apple Calendar access uses EventKit. Direct Google Calendar access uses Google authentication and Google’s Calendar API. Only selected calendars are included. Calendar requests and approved changes communicate with Google when that connection is used; calendar data is not sent to an AI provider. Aurora changes an event only after explicit review and approval, and does not automatically cancel appointments or notify attendees. You can disconnect Google in calendar settings and revoke access through Google’s account controls.

Storage and Sharing

Aurora stores app data locally on the device. Summary and CSV export features use the iOS share sheet and require explicit user action. The app does not create an Aurora cloud account or upload health logs or conversations to Aurora servers. Record and conversation storage is excluded from device backups; retained migration and recovery copies stay local until erased. There is no cross-device health or conversation sync. Google calendar access is the optional network integration described above.

Deleting Data

Settings offers separate controls to clear current recorded data, clear conversation, or Erase all local data. Full local erase removes records, conversation snapshots, retained migration/recovery copies, local calendar selections and Google credentials, preferences, and Aurora reminders. It does not delete Apple Health samples or events in external calendars, and does not delete exports you shared elsewhere. An interrupted erase is retried before local data can be reopened. Record-only clearing retains conversation and recovery copies; use full erase when you want those removed too.

Medical Disclaimer

Aurora is informational only. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or condition.
